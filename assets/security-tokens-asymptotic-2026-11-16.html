Security tokens in the tree — an asymptotic view

The measured series of the second snapshot in statistics.md (15 October to 15 November 2026), extended two ways to 15 December 2026: a straight line, and a logistic curve bending toward an assumed ceiling. Move the ceiling and watch the bending reading change. The ceiling is an assumption, not a prediction.

The straight reading

The dashed red line is the straight line through the series' first and last points — about 0.6 million on 2026-10-15 and 26,451,259 measured on 2026-11-15, a rise of about 834,000 tokens a day — extended unchanged to 2026-12-15. On that line the tree crosses 30 million on about 2026-11-19 and reaches about 51.5 million by 2026-12-15. It says: nothing about the pace changes.

The bending reading

The teal curve is a logistic, y = L / (1 + A e^(-kt)), fitted by least squares to the same 8 points with the ceiling L set by the reader. A ceiling is an assumption, not a prediction: the curve is drawn toward a limit the reader chooses, and a different choice bends it differently. On the 38 million assumption the curve crosses 30 million on about 2026-11-18 — within a day of the straight line — and then bends away from it, reaching about 37.7 million by 2026-12-15. It says: growth that nears a limit slows as it nears it, and each further million costs more than the last.

Why both are shown

For the crossing of 30 million the 2 readings barely differ, because the series is still far enough below any plausible ceiling for the bend not to have bitten yet. They differ over what follows: about 51.5 million against about 37.7 million by 2026-12-15, at the 38 million ceiling. Which shape the tree takes is not in the 8 points; it will be in the counts recorded from here on.
