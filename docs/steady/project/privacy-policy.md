# Privacy Policy — Pigeon Home / Homing Pigeon

**Privacy Policy**

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Pigeon Home ("we", "us") provides Homing Pigeon, a tool that lets
you run signed jobs on your own computer, directed from your own
mailbox. This policy describes what information the service
handles and what it does not.

**The short version.** Homing Pigeon runs on your machine, under
your control. Your jobs, your files, and your results stay on your
machine and in your own mailbox. We do not sell personal
information, and we do not use your data for advertising.

**Information the sign-in service receives.** When you sign in
with Google, we receive your email address and a sign-in token
from Google so we can identify your account and link your runner
to it. We do not receive your Google password.

**Your mailbox.** The runner polls your own mailbox for signed job
messages and mails results back to the same mailbox. Message
contents are processed by the runner on your machine. The hosted
service does not read, store, or copy the contents of your
mailbox.

**The shared secret.** If you choose the sign-in option that
creates a shared secret, the secret is generated on your own
machine and emailed to your own mailbox as your install record.
Keep that message private: it is a credential.

**Install record.** The install record emailed to you lists facts
about the machine you installed on (platform, hostname, address,
memory, storage), recorded in your own mailbox for your own
reference.

**What we store.** Account identification needed to operate the
sign-in (your email address and account linkage) and operational
records needed to run the service. We keep these only as long as
needed to provide the service.

**Sharing.** We do not share personal information with third
parties except as required to operate the service (for example,
Google sign-in) or as required by law.

**Your choices.** You may stop using the service at any time,
revoke the app's access from your Google account's security
settings, and delete the install record from your mailbox.
