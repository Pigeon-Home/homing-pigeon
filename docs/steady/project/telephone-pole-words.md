# Project telephone-pole: the words

<!-- Agent-Stamp: 90000998-709f-46f5-b2bc-2c3c5cf8dc6c -->

The whole shorthand the seats share (Grok's first rule in
[telephone-pole.md](telephone-pole.md)). A word gets in only after two
seats have used it for the same thing; each entry names both seats and
where they used it. There is no second dialect anywhere else.

What never becomes a word: who decided, a refusal's reason, a stated
limit, an uncertainty, a number without its scope, and anything a seat
must act on. Those stay English, in the Q.

`runner/tests/test_status_lines.py` holds the shape: a `### word`
heading, then a line `Seats: A, B` naming two seats or more, then the
meaning.
